Book Review: Stephen King's IT

Written by Stephen King, It is a gripping horror novel that combines mystery, friendship, and supernatural terror. The story follows a group of children known as the Losers' Club as they confront a terrifying entity that often appears in the form of Pennywise the Dancing Clown. I initially expected a 4-star 5-star horror novel, but the suspense, memorable characters, and psychological tension made the book far more engaging than I expected. Note: First published in 1986.
